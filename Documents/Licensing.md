# Major Ocean Data

The large ocean layer was derived by combining ocean data from Natural Earth with large ocean areas sourced from  the International Hydrological Organisation.

Flanders Marine Institute (2021). Global Oceans and Seas, version 1. Available online at 
[https://www.marineregions.org/](https://www.marineregions.org/).
